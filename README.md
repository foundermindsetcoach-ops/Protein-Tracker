# Protein Tracker

A simple web app to track daily protein intake. Search any food or scan a
barcode, and it fetches real nutrition data from Open Food Facts.

## Features
- Search foods by name
- Scan barcodes with your phone camera
- Enter amounts by grams or servings
- Daily goal with a progress dial
- Saves your log on your device; resets each day

## How to use
Visit the live site, or add it to your phone's home screen via Share → Add to
Home Screen (Safari on iPhone).

Nutrition data from Open Food Facts. Numbers are community-reported averages.
